# OpenArm v2.0 arms (vendored): Enactic's arm, Anvil's joint ranges and gripper

The arms of the `openarm_bimanual` robot profile (`../openarm_bimanual.yaml`): Enactic's OpenArm v2.0, a 7-DOF arm,
left and right, with the **Anvil OpenArm 2.0's joint ranges** (J1 excepted: Enactic's window) and **Anvil's
Customizable Gripper** on the wrist flange instead of OpenArm's pinch gripper. Sim only (the arm node has no OpenArm
hardware driver).

## Source and licence

- **The arm:** <https://github.com/enactic/openarm_description>, commit `c710e19a4789ff04bae6ae9a04b1aa39130b649f`
  (`build_urdf.UPSTREAM_COMMIT`). Apache License 2.0 (`LICENSE`, copied from upstream). The files here are a modified
  derivative work: see "What was changed".
- **The gripper** (`gripper/`): Anvil's Customizable Gripper, from Anvil's OpenArm v2 URDF (its hand and fine-tip
  fingers: visual meshes, masses, inertias, frames); Anvil publishes the gripper's CAD.
- **Not here: Anvil's arm.** Anvil's OpenArm v2 URDF and its arm meshes came without a licence, so none of Anvil's
  arm geometry, meshes or inertials is in this package: only its joint ranges (numbers Anvil states for its arm) and
  the gripper.

## How the URDFs are made

No xacro here (not installed, and not needed): upstream ships an expanded URDF,
`assets/robot/openarm_v2.0/urdf/example/v2.urdf`, autogenerated by xacro from `openarm_v20.urdf.xacro` with the
`presets/v2/default_bimanual.yaml` robot. `build_urdf.py` reads it and the gripper files:

    python -m robot_utils.robots.openarm.build_urdf /path/to/openarm_description   # a checkout of the commit above

`tests/test_openarm.py::test_urdfs_are_the_generators_output` regenerates them and compares (set
`OPENARM_DESCRIPTION` to the checkout).

## What was changed (relative to upstream)

- **One arm per file**, prefixes stripped (`openarm_left_link1` -> `link1`, `openarm_left_joint1` -> `joint1`). Left
  and right are **upstream's own** models (the left is OpenArm's reflection of the right, with its own joint axes).
  Upstream mirrors the left arm's base_link, link1, link2, link5 and link6 meshes by a mesh scale (`1 -1 1`); that
  scale is baked into `left_<name>.stl` (the only geometry change; test_openarm.py checks the arms mirror in y).
- Kept as upstream: links `base_link`, `link1`..`link6` with their inertials (masses, COMs, inertias), joints
  `joint1`..`joint7` with their origins and axes, the efforts (40, 40, 27, 27, 7, 7, 7 N m; Anvil's are the same).
- **The joint ranges** (`build_urdf.LIMITS`):

  | joint | left (deg) | right (deg) | from |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | J1 | -200..+80 | -80..+200 | Enactic's window |
  | J2 | -190..+10 | -10..+190 | Anvil (= Enactic's) |
  | J3 | -90..+90 | -90..+90 | Anvil (= Enactic's) |
  | J4 | 0..+140 | 0..+140 | Anvil (= Enactic's) |
  | J5 | -90..+90 | -90..+90 | Anvil (= Enactic's) |
  | **J6** | **-45..+70** | **-70..+45** | **Anvil** (Enactic: +-45) |
  | J7 | -90..+90 | -90..+90 | Anvil (= Enactic's) |

  In radians as Anvil states them (e.g. J6 left -0.785398..1.22173, J4 0..2.443461); J2-J5 and J7 differ from
  upstream's only in rounding. **Velocities are Anvil's**: 10.47 rad/s on J1-J2, 3.77 on J3-J4, 12.57 on J5-J7
  (upstream's 16.755 / 5.4454 / 20.944); the arm node caps far lower anyway (`arm_type.yaml` `joint_vel`).
- **Why Anvil's numbers carry over unchanged:** Anvil's OpenArm v2 is this arm. In Anvil's model, at q = 0, every
  joint origin lies within 1e-9 m of Enactic's and every joint axis, with its sign, is Enactic's (FK); so the same q is
  the same arm pose and a range means the same thing in both. `test_anvil_ranges_carry_over_by_fk` re-checks it, and
  that the gripper frames agree at random q, when `OPENARM_ANVIL_URDF_DIR` points at the maintainer's Anvil-derived
  URDFs (they are not distributed; the test skips otherwise).
- **J6, the sign:** Anvil's wider side (70 deg) tilts the fingertips **toward the wrist camera** (end_link +z: forward
  at the zero pose, up with the forearm forward) on both arms (`test_j6_wider_side_tilts_the_fingertips_toward_the_wrist_camera`).
  An earlier Enactic-based model here inferred the reverse sign (right -45..+70) from a human-wrist analogy; Anvil's
  URDF is authoritative.
- **J1 keeps Enactic's window** (right -80..+200 deg, left -200..+80; Anvil's is +-140 deg): the window's position is
  a mounting choice, not a joint property (both span 280 deg, centred 60 deg apart; re-clocking the J1 housing or
  another mounting plate shifts it), and Enactic's keeps the overhead forward reach (200 vs 140 deg forward), which
  matters more for human teleop than Anvil's 60 deg more backward (the owner's choice, 2026-11-25).
- `link7`: joint 7's child, a massless flange (1 g, a tiny inertia: simulators need one), carrying the **flange
  adapter** (`build_urdf.ADAPTER`: a 50 x 50 x 22.6 mm box, visual and collision, no mass of its own, an ESTIMATE). It
  fills the gap between Enactic's wrist housing (link6's mesh ends 28.5 mm below the joint-7 axis) and the top of
  Anvil's hand (51.1 mm below it). Upstream's pinch gripper is removed.
- Meshes: the collision STLs serve as the visual meshes too (upstream's visual meshes are COLLADA, which MuJoCo
  cannot load).

## The gripper (`gripper/`, `../grippers.py` `anvil`)

- **Frames, our convention:** `end_link` at the fingertips (the fine tips' far end), midway between the jaws, x out
  of the fingertips, y the jaw travel, z toward the wrist camera; `finger_left` prismatic along end_link +y,
  `finger_right` its mimic (-1); each finger link's frame is end_link's at q = 0 (the jaw boxes and the arm node's
  finger frames assume it). The arm node's gripper law, the jaw boxes and the sim's gripper model apply as for the
  reBot's gripper.
- **The mount** (`build_urdf.END_FROM_J7`): end_link 0.2026 m below the joint-7 axis along the flange's normal, x
  along link7's -z: where Anvil's model puts it relative to joint 7 (Anvil's and Enactic's link7 frames differ by a
  constant rotation; FK at q = 0 and random q: 3e-9 m, 4e-6 rad). 39.6 mm of that is Enactic's flange (upstream's
  pinch gripper's top face).
- **Stroke:** 0..0.05 m per finger, 100 mm of gap (Anvil's URDF; Anvil's docs say 0-110 mm, perhaps the standard
  fingers'). The motor law keeps Anvil's documented 0 .. -2.858 rad (a DM-J4310-2EC): 35.0 mm of gap per motor rad.
- **Masses and inertias:** the hand 0.7 kg (with the wrist camera), each finger 0.036 kg, Anvil's tensors.
- **The TCP:** Anvil's `hand_tcp`, 22.5 mm behind the fingertips (the profile's `tcp`).
- **Force:** Anvil's 150 N rated / 350 N max (docs, both fingers): the arm node's squeeze 75 N per jaw by default,
  175 N at most (an inference). The URDF's finger effort (10 N) and velocity (1 m/s) are placeholders.
- **The wrist camera:** Anvil's `wrist_cam` frame, in end_link (-0.0935, -0.001, 0.074) m, looking out along end_link
  x tilted 47 deg down onto the jaws (the profile's `wrist_camera` mount).
- **Collisions:** the gripper type's boxes (hand: housing, guide, camera arm, camera; fingers: the jaw boxes), the
  same boxes the sim's grasp contact (`world.py` `_jaws`) and the block views use; the meshes are visual only.

## Masses

Enactic's links (base_link..link6: 5.36 kg per arm), the 1 g flange, the gripper 0.77 kg (hand + fingers): 6.13 kg
per arm. The maintainer's Anvil model weighs 6.43-6.44 kg (Anvil's own link inertials, its 0.24 kg link7).

## The arm type (`arm_type.yaml`)

- MIT gains: OpenArm's own (Enactic's `control_gains.yaml`).
- Torque limits: the joint efforts above, the peak torques of the Damiao motors they match (DM8009 joints 1-2, DM4340
  3-4, DM4310 5-7; inferred from the efforts).
- The arm node's speed / acceleration caps: our policy (2 rad/s on joints 1-4, 6 rad/s on 5-7), below the URDF's
  velocity limits.
- Jam bands, the sim's per-joint friction / armature (in the robot profile): ESTIMATES.
- `geometry`: the shoulder / elbow / wrist points (joint origins 2 / 4 / 6) and the upper-arm / forearm capsules of the
  WBC's human-like posture and arm-proximity term (`nodes/wbc/humanlike.py`).
